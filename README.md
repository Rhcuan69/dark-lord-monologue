My entry for the make.fun bounty "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain
Monologue" (u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb):
https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb

One program, pure Python standard library, writes a single 100-word
monologue to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, nothing read from outside the
repository, deterministic — every run produces the same bytes.

The speaker is a specific villain with a history and a scheme: a crown taken
off a dead king's head at Greyford, nine winters of petitions, a mirror that
drinks one dream from every village each month, a census, a headsman. The
tiredness is never announced — it lives in the pauses, the cold tea, the
squeaking hinge, and an execution rescheduled to after breakfast. The sting
lands in the last line.

## The monologue

> You want the speech. Fine. Stand there, by the — no, not the tapestry, it sheds. Right. I took this crown off a dead king's head at Greyford, and I have worn it through nine winters of petitions and one determined badger. My mirror drinks one dream from every village each month. That is the law. That— hm. The tea is cold again. Tell the rebellion I will crush it on Thursday, after the census, and someone oil this hinge. It squeaks like a— like a— The headsman comes at dawn. No — after breakfast. I want a front seat.
